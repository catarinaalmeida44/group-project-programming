# The music of the game

Five recorded tunes, chosen by Catarina on 20 November 2026. They replaced the songs made of notes by our own code (`client/music-score.js`), which only the sound check page still plays.

Which tune plays when is decided in `client/music-files.js`. `client/music.js` plays them.

| File here | When it plays | Its name where it came from | Made by | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `menu.mp3` | The main menu, and an online room before a race. Round and round | `racing_game_menu_bpm165.mp3` ("Racing Game Menu") | MintoDog, on OpenGameArt | CC0 |
| `lap-first.mp3` | The first lap of a race. Round and round | `Race Finish Bad (Loop)_BPM100.mp3`, from "Racing Game Finish & Jingle" | MintoDog, on OpenGameArt | CC0 |
| `lap-middle.mp3` | The laps between the first and the last. Round and round | `Race Finish Good (Loop)_BPM130.mp3`, from the same pack | MintoDog, on OpenGameArt | CC0 |
| `lap-last.mp3` | The last lap, and on until the podium. Round and round | `Race Finish Best (Loop)_BPM132.mp3`, from the same pack | MintoDog, on OpenGameArt | CC0 |
| `podium.flac` | The podium: trumpets and clapping. Once | `end_level.flac` | "YoFrankie! (c) 2008, Blender Foundation, www.blender.org" | CC-BY 3.0 |

- **CC0** means the maker gave the music to everybody: it can be used and passed on without asking and without naming them. We name them anyway.
- **CC-BY 3.0** means it can be used and passed on if the maker is named. That is why the line in quotes above is also in the credits of the main `README.md`. It must stay there for as long as the file is used.

The other files of the "Racing Game Finish & Jingle" pack are not used and are not in this repository.

The files are as they were downloaded: nothing was cut or changed. Only their names are ours.
