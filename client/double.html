Two drivers on one keyboard. Player 1, on the left: W gas, S brake and reverse, A D steer. Player 2, on the right: ↑ gas, ↓ brake and reverse, ← → steer. R starts again. V changes the camera of both halves. Drive clockwise. M switches the sound off and on: it is on. Game settings chooses whether computer karts race with you and how good they are. F puts the game on the whole screen, and back (Esc also leaves it).

A race for two on the test track. It runs in your browser: nothing is sent to our server, and nothing is saved, neither for your stats nor for the leaderboard. The race is over when every kart has finished, or two minutes after the first one has. Some keyboards cannot tell more than a few keys apart when they are held at the same time: if a key seems to be ignored, let go of another one.
