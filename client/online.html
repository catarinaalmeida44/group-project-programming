    Arrow keys: ↑ gas, ↓ brake and reverse, ← → steer. V changes the camera. Drive clockwise. Enter starts a race for everyone, for the host only. Before a race, R puts your kart back at the start. The yellow light on the road is the checkpoint to drive through next.

    Everyone who opens this page is in the same room on the test track, and our server moves all the karts. The host is whoever has been here longest. If you arrive during a race, you watch it and join at the next start. Karts bump into each other. A race that has started is driven to its end: it is over when everyone has finished, or one minute after the first kart has finished. If you are signed in, the server saves your time when you finish a race, and it appears on the leaderboard in the menu.
