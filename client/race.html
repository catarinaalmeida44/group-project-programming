Arrow keys: ↑ gas, ↓ brake and reverse, ← → steer. R starts again. V changes the camera. Drive clockwise. M switches the sound off and on: it is on. Game settings above the track chooses whether computer karts race with you and how good they are (Easy, Normal or Hard), and starts the race again. F puts the game on the whole screen, and back (Esc also leaves it).

A race on the test track. It runs in your browser. Your best times are kept in this browser. If you are signed in, a finished race also counts in your own stats (your name in the menu), but never for the shared leaderboard. A race without computer karts does not count there either: there is nobody to beat.
